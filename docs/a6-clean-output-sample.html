Wisdom Rain A6 Clean Mode

This document demonstrates the sanitized HTML structure used by the WRPR reader after Mammoth output is cleaned.

Inline Emphasis

Inline bold highlights remain, but full-paragraph bold wrappers are removed.

Single emphasis paragraphs render with lighter weight to avoid overpowering the layout.

Unordered List

  • Lead item text continues normally after the initial strong keyword.
  • Regular list entries keep spacing without stray styles.
  • Nested emphasis stays inline without wrapping the whole row.

Ordered List

  1. Numeric steps keep their structure while bold wrappers are normalized.
  2. Attributes such as width, height, class, and inline styles are stripped.
  3. Empty paragraphs and break tags are removed to prevent pagination gaps.

Compact Paragraphs

Paragraphs keep a consistent bottom margin of 14px to align with the A6 layout grid.

Heading spacing is tuned for readability within the fixed-height reader viewport.
